Welcome to Gielinor

A short guide before you step into Lumbridge.

  • WASD walks. Left click the ground, a monster, or a shop.
  • Right drag orbits the camera. The compass resets it behind you.
  • F1–F6 open combat, skills, inventory, equipment, prayer, and magic.
  • The tracker under your health shows the next quest step and your Slayer task.
  • You start with nothing. Everything you wear is earned.
  • First steps: follow the tracker. Six short tasks in the haven pay your first kit.
