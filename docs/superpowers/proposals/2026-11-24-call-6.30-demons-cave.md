# CALL-6.30: the demons' cave, proposal (awaiting the owner)

Drafted 2026-11-24 for the owner decision recorded at the top of the content-systems design. Recommended package, pending the owner's answers to the questions below:

- **The pass sentries stay.** The four lesser demons at (-42..-22, 55) are `pass_sentries`, the gate of Cinderstep Pass (`worldMap.ts` 89-98, 225-230). Chapter 5 ("The Held Pass") is built on them. Six frozen or Stage 4 tests pin them:
  - `gate-simulation` 32-33 and 95-104;
  - `world-data` 263 and 325;
  - `monster-patterns` 492-493;
  - `sustain` 224-225.
- **Only `slagfall_terrace` moves** (`worldMap.ts` 230-233). It holds the two demons at (-86, 96) and (-78, 104). They move into a **standing cave**:
  - their own zone at origin (1000, 600), per design 6.4 option A;
  - they respawn, with timers that run only while the hero is inside;
  - they keep full sight;
  - there is no boss, no chest and no reset;
  - each demon keeps its own drop chances.
- **One door at (-84, 98)**, the centre of the terrace pad. Distances from the door:

  | Feature | Distance from the door |
  |---|---|
  | Dragon camp leash | 9.1 clear |
  | Ashmantle's arena margin | 29.5 clear |
  | Underkiln door | 61.8 |
  | Runite rock | 11.7 |
  | Region safe circle | 19.1 clear |

  The terrace becomes the empty cave mouth, framed by its two obsidian spires. No new name is added.
- **Numbers.**
  - Unchanged:
    - the 7.6 baseline of 178.5 GP/s (it reads the largest demon camp, the sentries' 4);
    - the pacing model (by hand; rerun once to confirm);
    - the diary rows (they count kills by type);
    - the gate simulation.
  - Changed:
    - main's member count goes from 42 to 40, so Stage 6 ends at 46, not 48;
    - declared replacements 4 and 5 change only in their numbers.
  - The cave's camps live in the zone table, not in `CAMPS`.
- **Look.** Basalt floor tiles, charcoal wall boxes about 6 high, and no ceiling.
  - `World_lava_fissure` seams along the walls glow ember.
  - Obsidian spires stand as pillars.
  - A fixed **cave** light frame in `look.ts` replaces the day cycle while the hero is in the zone:
    - a dim ember key light;
    - a near-black violet fill;
    - a strong orange bounce;
    - dense red-brown fog;
    - exposure about 1.2.
  - Sky, sun, moon, clouds, weather and carpet are hidden. A few ash motes stay.
  - The saved clock keeps running.
  - Stage 5 has no zone concept, so Stage 6 adds this hook.
  - The lava cannot use point lights, because the lower tiers have none. The glow comes from the bounce and from emissive seams, which bloom.
- **Camera.** Inside the cave, the arm shortens wherever it would leave the room rectangles.

Alternatives considered:
- **The cave as a third dungeon run.** It needs a boss with a new model, which Stage 6 forbids, and it moves the 7.6 income baseline.
- **The whole pass as a cave.** It breaks the six tests above and removes the gate.
- **A weaker camp on the terrace.** It reopens CALL-6.13.
- **A guard post at the mouth.** It breaks G6.6, because the door would sit inside a leash.
