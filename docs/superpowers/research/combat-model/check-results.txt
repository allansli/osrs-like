2000 seeded fights per row; positions, the click back onto the boss and the walk back are modelled.
Ranged and magic heroes stand 11 from the boss (its own reach) and attack from anywhere within 13.5.
The level-50 rows have no ward, no protection prayer and 10 lobsters. "Never moves" ignores every shape.
